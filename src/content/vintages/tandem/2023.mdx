Really light color; smelled bright, crisp, whiffs of earth and maybe strawberry
skin? like almost woody, husky, alive.

Nicely tannic, not overpowering. Jammy through line, like spiced fruits,
cinnamon, apricot, chai-adjacent almost. Unusually light-bodied, I feel those
sort of flavors tend to come with something fuller-bodied.

Really nice after decanting for a while, like it settled in, had an almost
buttery or cheesy feel to it, in the sense that it seemed to have coalesced,
developed this smooth funk.

Had been told to pair it with a steak, along with
[Giacomo Fenocchio's _Langhe Freisa_](/wines/langhe-freisa). That this would be
overpowered, but to start with it, then come back to it after eating. And sure
enough, a nice pairing to my meal, but drowned out, more a background complement
than an even pairing. Contrasting in lightness, not matching the food's
intensity. But it was nice to retry after the meal, its lightness refreshing,
palette-cleansing
