Smelled clean, like Blockbuster or Staples, a corporate office or unboxed video
game console, off-gassing, fresh, hard to place, but nostalgic, pleasant. Deep,
not fruity or spicy.

Really liked this. Buttery, rich, dense, tannic behind the teeth and the sides
of your mouth. Paired with steak: exquisite. Soaked into the meat, amplified
richness, like the steak growing with the wine.

One of my favorites so far.
