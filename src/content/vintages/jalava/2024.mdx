Really cool, light, cloudy color. Like slightly milky ruby grapefruit juice.
Never seen a wine that color before. Smelled mellow, mellon-y, fresh, the smell
of, I always say like plastic-fresh-office-supplies-off-gassing sort of smell
(which, to be clear, I really enjoy). It smelled low-alochol (only 11.2%)

Taste, so light, like apple skin, bright, but not really acidic, though with a
pinch / spiciness / tartness / I'm-not-sure-what at the end.
