Intense!

Strong smell, red fruit-y, vibrant, clean, fresh, plastic-y or ink-y almost,
like the wafting off a sharpee, that sticks-in-your-nostrils sort of smell. On a
swirl, deeper, chocolate almost, hint of lemon peel.

Taste, very tannic, pinch in the nose, you feel the higher alcohol content.
Gripping. Taste reminiscent of the smell of fading lemon peel.

Liked this a lot.
