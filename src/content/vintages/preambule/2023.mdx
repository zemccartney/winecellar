The best apple juice you've ever had. Just sweet enough, just tart enough.
Really, really liked this.

Bought this on recommendation from the folks at Maine and Loire, who told me
some of the background of pèt-nats (stands for Pètillant Naturel
[translates to "naturally bubbling"](https://petnat.ch/what-is-pet-nat/)), two
highlights:

- [Disgorgement](https://www.yumewine.co/learn/2023/8/3/disgorging-pet-nat-unveiling-the-fizz-with-style).
  Rad
- Learning of
  [Christian Chaussard](https://www.naturalwineco.com/producer/domaine-le-briseau-100615/),
  who sparked interest in the style after just kinda stumbling on it, when he
  accidentally let some wine re-ferment in the bottle. How it seems to go with
  so many great discoveries
